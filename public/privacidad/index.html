Privacy & contact notice

When you submit the AB Studio inquiry form, we attempt to store your name, email, requested service and description in our private CRM to respond and follow up about your request. The form tells you if storage fails. WhatsApp or your email client opens separately, and you choose whether to send a message there. We do not sell your inquiry details. Authorized team members can access them. Our hosting and database providers process information to deliver the service. To request access or deletion of your inquiry, email sales.abstudio@gmail.com. We retain inquiries only as needed for follow-up and legitimate business records, subject to applicable requirements.

En español

No vendemos la información de las consultas. Solo el equipo autorizado puede acceder a ella. Nuestros proveedores de alojamiento y base de datos procesan datos para prestar el servicio. Para solicitar acceso o eliminación de tu consulta, escribe a sales.abstudio@gmail.com. Conservamos las solicitudes solo durante el tiempo necesario para seguimiento y registros comerciales legítimos, según los requisitos aplicables.
