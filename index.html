Notes from the studio

Ideas for a more useful web.

Short, practical notes for business owners thinking about their website, digital tools and online visibility.

Web strategy

What should a small business website do?

Start with the customer’s next step, not a long list of features.

Read the note

A useful site quickly explains what you offer, who it helps and how to take the next step. Put the most important service, location and contact option where visitors can find them easily on a phone.

Booking systems

When is it time to add online reservations?

A booking flow helps when requests are frequent and details get lost.

Read the note

If your team often repeats the same questions about dates, times or availability, a structured form can collect those details in one place. Decide who confirms each request and how changes or cancellations will be handled before automating the flow.

Local visibility

A first step toward showing up in local search

Consistent business details make it easier for customers to recognize you.

Read the note

Check that your business name, phone number, hours and service area are accurate on your website and Google Business Profile. Keep the same details wherever customers find you, then add useful answers to the questions they ask most.
